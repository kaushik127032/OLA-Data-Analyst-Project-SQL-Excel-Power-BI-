## Project Overview

This project analyzes OLA ride booking operations to identify booking trends, cancellation behavior, revenue performance, customer activity, and driver performance. SQL was used to query and analyze booking data, Excel was used for data cleaning and preprocessing, and Power BI was used to build a business-ready interactive dashboard.

## Business Problem

Ride-hailing companies need to monitor booking performance, cancellations, revenue, and customer experience in real time. High cancellation rates and operational inefficiencies can reduce customer satisfaction and revenue. This project helps identify areas where operational improvements can increase ride completion rates and business performance.

## Key Insights

* Successful rides contribute the majority of completed bookings.
* Customer and driver cancellation patterns reveal operational bottlenecks.
* Vehicle type performance varies significantly across booking volume and revenue.
* UPI and digital payment methods account for a large share of completed transactions.
* Frequent customers generate a significant portion of booking revenue.
* Monitoring ride completion, cancellation rates, and customer ratings helps improve overall service quality.
